Debt Management Mobile App

A mobile product concept for recording debts and following payment activity.

Mobile App UX Research UI Design Hackathon Project

Project Details

Managing personal debt can be overwhelming and stressful. Users often struggle to keep track of multiple creditors, payment deadlines, and the overall progress of their repayment journey. The goal was to create a tool that simplifies this process while maintaining a sense of calm and control. We focused on a clean, minimalist interface that prioritizes clarity. By using a soft color palette and structured data visualization, we transformed complex financial information into an intuitive experience that encourages regular engagement and financial mindfulness.
