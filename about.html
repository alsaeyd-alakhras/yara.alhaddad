01 - PERSPECTIVE

"Good interfaces begin with understanding the system behind them."

My background in software engineering fundamentally shapes how I design. I don't just look at the visual layer; I deconstruct the underlying architecture. This allows me to create interfaces that are not only aesthetically pleasing but structurally sound and highly feasible for development teams to implement.

I believe that true simplicity on the front-end is achieved by rigorous analysis on the back-end. By mastering system flows and requirements early on, I ensure that the user's path remains clear, logical, and undisturbed by technical complexities.
